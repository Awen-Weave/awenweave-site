# Ffestiniog: engagement pack

**Ffestiniog** (community W04000063), in Gwynedd. Jobs and firms are for **the wider area (W02000018)**, which holds Ffestiniog, never split to the community. Community census figures are **approximate**. Every figure is marked [S…] (a served claim) or [R…] (a report section), listed at the foot. English text. The Welsh version of this page is to follow.

## The three strongest opportunities

Llefelys's reading of the evidence, by a rule fixed before computing; not a forecast or a recommendation.

1. **Arts, entertainment, recreation & other services**: the wider area's highest location quotient (2.7 [S15]) among the sectors whose Gwynedd jobs grew 2015 to 2024 (by 500 [R1]†).
2. **Kitchen and catering assistants**: the found skills route with the most Gwynedd adverts (224 [R2]): kitchen assistant into kitchen porter, 10 of 13 essential skills shared.
3. **Public contracts won outside Wales** (rule (c): the CPV division where most Gwynedd buyers' awards go to suppliers outside Wales): awaiting the publisher's licence answer. Its figures will come from Find a Tender once a served layer gives them.

## Skills routes

Of the proposal's routes, one was found (energy work) and 5 were not (drone work, retrofit work, aquaculture, offshore energy, care). Of Gwynedd's five most-advertised occupations, 4 have a short route from an occupation the proposal names and 1 has none (Care workers and home carers) [R4].

## Questions to put

- **To the council:** Arts, entertainment, recreation & other services is more concentrated in the wider area than in Wales (quotient 2.7 [S15]) and grew in Gwynedd [R1]: what in the council's plans helps it keep growing here?
- **To the North Wales Regional Skills Partnership:** Kitchen and catering assistants had the most Gwynedd adverts (224 [R2]) and a short route into it exists: is training for it offered near Ffestiniog?
- **To a funder:** skilled trades are the occupations north Wales employers most often find hard to fill (33%; Wales 30% [R3]): would you back local training or apprenticeships in them?
